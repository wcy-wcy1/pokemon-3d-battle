Pikachu

Pikachu is a Pokémon that excels at attacking opponents from far away with electricity. Sometimes the attacks even leave opposing Pokémon paralyzed!

Attacker

Ranged

Difficulty: Novice

  • Offense 4.5
  • Endurance 1.5
  • Mobility 2.5
  • Scoring 2
  • Support 1.5

Pikachu’s Unite Move is Thunderstorm, an attack that blasts all surrounding opposing Pokémon with powerful lightning.
